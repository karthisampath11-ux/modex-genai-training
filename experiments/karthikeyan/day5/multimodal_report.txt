*   **Dominance of Electronics:** The "Top Products" list indicates a strong and consistent demand for electronic gadgets, particularly audio devices and smart wearables.

    *   **Recommendation:** Based on the popularity of the top products, develop strategies for cross-selling and upselling.
    *   **Reasoning:** Customers buying headphones might be interested in portable chargers, or those buying smartwatches might be interested in fitness bands.
