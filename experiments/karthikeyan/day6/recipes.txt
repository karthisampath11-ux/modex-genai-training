Classic Tomato Pasta serves 2 people. Ingredients include 200 g pasta, 150 g tomato sauce, 10 ml olive oil, 2 pieces garlic, and 5 g basil. Boil the pasta, heat sauce with garlic and oil, mix everything together, and garnish with basil. Prep time is about 25 minutes.

Simple Vegetable Sandwich serves 1 person. Ingredients include 2 pieces bread, 50 g cucumber, 50 g tomato, 20 g cheese, and 1 tbsp mayonnaise. Spread mayonnaise on bread, add vegetables and cheese, close the sandwich, and toast lightly. Prep time is 10 minutes.

Banana Smoothie serves 2 people. Ingredients include 2 pieces banana, 250 ml milk, 1 tbsp honey, and 4 pieces ice cubes. Add all ingredients to a blender, blend until smooth, and serve chilled. Prep time is about 5 minutes.

Lemon Rice serves 3 people. Ingredients include 300 g cooked rice, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 10 pieces curry leaves, and 20 ml oil. Heat oil, add mustard seeds and curry leaves, mix with rice, add lemon juice, and stir well. Prep time is 15 minutes.

Chocolate Mug Cake serves 1 person. Ingredients include 4 tbsp flour, 2 tbsp cocoa powder, 2 tbsp sugar, 3 tbsp milk, and 1 tbsp oil. Mix all ingredients in a mug, microwave for 2 minutes, and serve warm. Prep time is about 7 minutes.
